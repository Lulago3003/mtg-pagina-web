Frequently asked questions

If your question isn't here, message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply the same business day.

How much is the Smart Meter?

The roughly one-month diagnosis is USD 150. Permanent monitoring is USD 425, currently on promotion at USD 350. Monthly follow-up is optional at USD 11.99 a month.

Does it work with ENSA, EDEMET and EDECHI?

Yes. The sensor measures your internal panel whatever the utility, and we read ENSA, EDEMET, EDECHI and Naturgy bills.

How much will I save?

It depends on which appliances use energy in your property, which is exactly what we measure. We don't promise percentages: we give you the findings and what each cut is worth on your tariff.

Do the watches need a SIM?

The 4G watches do: a nano SIM with data and calls. The S-F3 and S-ET488 health watches connect to your phone by Bluetooth and need no SIM. We help you get everything working.

Is there a monthly fee for the app?

No. Each watch uses the manufacturer's free app for iPhone and Android. The only monthly cost is the SIM plan.

Do you handle large fiber and telecom projects?

Yes. We supply passive and active fiber, antennas, structures and KHIPU data centers, with installation and certification. Send us the bill of materials or the drawing and we'll quote it.

Do you rent test equipment?

Yes. OTDRs, optical power meters, light sources and fiber microscopes, for sale or rent. Ask about availability for your job dates.

How do I request support?

Message us on WhatsApp at 6887-3065, Monday to Friday 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. We reply the same day. Customers with a maintenance contract get preventive visits and priority.
